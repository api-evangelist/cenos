Induction shrink fitting relies on rapid, localized thermal expansion to achieve very tight interference fits for high-precision tooling. In tool holder assemblies, achieving the necessary radial clearance to insert or extract a cutting tool requires precise electromagnetic power delivery. However, induction heating creates steep, non-uniform thermal gradients.
